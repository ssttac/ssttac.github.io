Society of Saint Theodore of Tarsus

Archbishop of Canterbury

The Society of Saint Theodore of Tarsus, Archbishop of Canterbury is a digital ministry dedicated to glorifying God and bringing open-source digital resources to Anglicans worldwide. The creation of the Society was inspired by the lack of free (as in libre) content available for the use of prayer, research, or creative projects.

Currently, this is a one-man initiative, but I am looking for more contributors.

The Society is not affiliated with any church in particular, but generally seeks to be aligned with the Anglican Communion and Episcopal Church of the United States, and High Church Anglo-Catholicism.

The Society is inspired by the spirit of the 1979 Book of Common Prayer, whose copyright was voluntarily relinquished on its adoption; and Saint Theodore of Tarsus who travelled from the other side of the known world to become Archbishop of Canterbury from 668 to 690. He introduced much knowledge and learning to the Western church.
